Who I Am

I'm Kehinde Odewabi, a Zoology graduate (2024) with a background in vector ecology and field research. My undergraduate thesis investigated mosquito breeding site distribution in Nigeria which gave me a deep appreciation for how environmental and socioeconomic factors shape disease risk. I now channel that scientific curiosity into public health data analytics, using tools like Power BI, Tableau, SQL, and Excel to turn complex health datasets into clear, actionable insights. My goal is a Master's degree in Health Informatics, where I can bridge field science, epidemiology, and data systems at scale.
